How CineML Works

CineML couples a scalable MongoDB Atlas database with an offline Machine Learning pipeline to deliver fast, privacy-preserving recommendations without user tracking.

🍃

MongoDB Atlas

Stores movie documents, genres, plot metadata, and poster URLs for flexible querying and instant retrieval.

🔤

TF-IDF Vectorization

Plot overviews, genres, and keywords are tokenized, stemmed, and converted into high-dimensional numerical vectors.

📐

Cosine Distance

Evaluates the angular proximity between movie content vectors to measure narrative and thematic similarity.
